Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account (email, name) and when registering domains (WHOIS contact data mandated by ICANN). We do not collect or store your search queries.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information to provision your domains, communicate with you about your services, and comply with legal requirements (such as ICANN data escrow). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies strictly necessary for maintaining your session state (like Dark Mode preferences and authentication). We do not use third-party invasive trackers.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption (AES-256) to protect your data. Payment data is processed directly by our PCI-compliant providers; we never store your credit card information on our servers.

5. Your Rights

If you reside in the EU (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data. Please contact privacy@aidomains.com to exercise these rights.
